## 🧩 About

**Far Cry 6 Companion** is a curated list of Far Cry 6 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, HUD customizer, waypoint switcher
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — inventory catalog, weapon manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — mission layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Gameplay Helpers
- Waypoint Switcher — travel to any discovered location
- Show Collectibles — visualize hidden items and chests
- Replay Notes — record and review mission runs
- Playback Speed — adjust game speed for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Item Catalog — browse weapons, outfits, vehicles, and gear
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize your favorite loadouts
- HUD Visibility — toggle HUD elements for a cleaner view

### 🏋️ Tactical Layout
- Stealth Notes — visualize enemy patrols and vision cones
- Resource Tracker — locate crafting materials and ammo
- Fast Travel Notes — unlock and manage fast travel points
